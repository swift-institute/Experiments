    SeamKit            (declares __M7BufferProtocol + unconstrained isEmpty default)

    Witnesses          (six conformers; conform __M7BufferProtocol across the boundary)
       ^  imported by
    adt-tower-m7-concretize-count  (executable; the generic `report(_:_:)`

unconstrained protocol-extension default) on values whose conformances are
witnessed in the *separate* Witnesses module. This is genuine cross-module
generic dispatch over the seam, not a same-module pass.
